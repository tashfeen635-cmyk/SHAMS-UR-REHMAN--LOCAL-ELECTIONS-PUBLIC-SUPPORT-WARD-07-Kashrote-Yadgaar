Delete ALL survey data?

This is irreversible. Every survey submission will be permanently deleted from the database, including respondent names, YES/NO responses, and timestamps. Dashboard totals, charts, percentages, and reports will reset to zero.

Admin accounts, admin sessions, website settings, and campaign assets will not be deleted.
